💀 Skull

  • Made of 22 fused bones.
  • Protects the brain and forms your face.
  • The jaw (mandible) is the only movable bone here!

🦴 Clavicle

  • Commonly known as the collarbone.
  • It is the most frequently broken bone in the body!
  • Connects your arms to your main skeleton.

🩻 Ribcage

  • Most people have 12 pairs of ribs (24 total).
  • Protects your heart and lungs like a cage.
  • The bottom two pairs are called "floating ribs."

💪 Humerus

  • The long bone of your upper arm.
  • Connects your shoulder to your elbow.
  • Hitting your "funny bone" is actually hitting a nerve near this bone!

🦾 Radius & Ulna

  • The twin bones of your forearm.
  • The Radius is always on the thumb-side of your arm.
  • They cross over each other when you turn your hand over.

🦴 Pelvis

  • A basin-shaped structure that supports your upper body.
  • Connects your spine to your legs.
  • Male and female pelvises are shaped slightly differently.

🖐️ Phalanges

  • The bones that make up your fingers AND toes.
  • You have 14 in each hand.
  • Your thumb has 2 phalanges, but your other fingers have 3!

🦵 Femur

  • The longest and strongest bone in the body.
  • It makes up about one-quarter of your total height!
  • It takes immense force to break this bone.

🛡️ Patella

  • Commonly known as your kneecap.
  • Babies are born without bone here (it's cartilage at first!).
  • Acts like a shield for your knee joint.

🦵 Tibia & Fibula

  • The Tibia is your shinbone and bears most of your weight.
  • The Fibula is much thinner and mostly used for attaching muscles.
